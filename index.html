🌱 Ms. Heavy Metal Leaf: The Bio-Cyborg Project

Ms. Heavy Metal Leaf is a hyperaccumulator bio-cyborg sensorbot system—a groundbreaking project being developed alongside my collaborators.

What are Hyperaccumulators and Phytomining?
Hyperaccumulator plants are unique botanical organisms capable of absorbing extremely high concentrations of heavy metals (like nickel, zinc, and copper) from the soil into their roots, stems, and leaves through a natural process called phytomining.

The Growth & Sensor Integration:
She is grown directly inside a specialized clear mold embedded with micro-sensors and system components. Because the plant grows naturally into the shape and structure of the mold, there is zero messy installation required—the living biology and the electronic matrix form as one unified organism.

Our Core Objective:
We are setting out to definitively prove that hyperaccumulator plants can be successfully utilized for growing sustainable, living circuit board frames and electronic hardware components from the ground up.
