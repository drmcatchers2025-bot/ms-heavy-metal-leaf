ROOTS IN THE RED DESERT & BEYOND

Growing up on the high desert of the Hopi and Navajo reservation wasn’t just a childhood—it was a masterclass in resilience and self-reliance. As a white kid in a large, tight-knit family of fifteen brothers and sisters, my world wasn't quiet. It was dusty, hard, and absolutely the best.

We didn't flip switches for light or turn handles for running water. Instead, our home was a monument to sweat and stubborn hope: a sturdy house built with our own hands out of rock and mud. Our lifeline was a creaking windmill that pumped water from deep beneath the earth, and our rhythm was dictated by the sun, the desert wind, and the expansive Arizona sky. Living off-grid taught me early on how to adapt, how to create with very little, and how to find strength in family amid the harsh, beautiful landscape.

Eventually, the horizon called me westward to the contrasting energy of California. Life took its turns, bringing marriage and a later move out to the sweeping plains of North Dakota. But chapters close, and before my marriage ended, I faced one of my biggest trials when I survived a life-threatening infection that brought me face-to-face with mortality.

Following my divorce in 2017, I faced another monumental battle and permanently ended my addiction to alcohol, stepping out into a brand-new kind of clarity and freedom.

Today, I’ve put down anchor in Bremerton, Washington—close enough to my mom and sisters to share the everyday moments that matter most. The desert sands and the windmill are years behind me now, but every trial I’ve walked through has only deepened the grit forged in that rock-and-mud house, and I carry it with me into everything I do.

The Hyperaccumulator Project

Born from that deep resilience, Ms. Heavy Metal Leaf is an industrial-art ecosystem and digital reclamation project. Just like hyperaccumulator plants in nature that draw toxic heavy metals out of contaminated earth to heal the soil, this system draws from the raw, heavy frequencies of life: survival, addiction, recovery, and reinvention. It is a fusion of uncompromising heavy metal soundscapes, Suno-powered audio transmissions, digital oracles, and custom-designed merchandise forged from the subterranean grid.
